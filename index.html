Cyber-Physical Coordination and Vision-Guided UAV for Urban Search and Rescue Operations

Final-year project, individual

An autonomous quadcopter for urban search and rescue. It flies planned missions, detects people and objectives with computer vision, and streams results to a ground-station dashboard so a rescue team can see what the aircraft sees. It began as an F450 airframe built and tuned from scratch.

  • Person and objective detection. YOLOv8 finds people and mission objectives in the live camera feed with above 90% accuracy.
  • Autonomous navigation. The aircraft flies planned paths and avoids obstacles with 95% path accuracy, using RPLidar SLAM, an A* path planner and a 15-state mission state machine.
  • Face recognition. DeepFace helps identify the people who need rescue.
  • Scene and condition analysis. A vision-language model describes the environment and the condition of the person who needs rescue.
  • Safe architecture. Safety-critical commands travel over SiK radio and everything else over Wi-Fi. Return-to-launch on Wi-Fi loss was verified in field tests.
  • Full stack. Pixhawk 4 with ArduPilot, a Raspberry Pi 5 companion computer, ROS 2 with MAVROS, and a Flask backend for live mission monitoring.

Sub-0.5 m path error and about 99% detection precision in field tests
